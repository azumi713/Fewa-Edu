Our Story

A Premier Japanese Language Institute, Built in Pokhara Since 2017

Fewa Kokusai Educational Consultancy Pvt. Ltd. is a Nepal Government-registered and approved consultancy offering Japanese language courses, exam preparation, and university placement support. Founded in 2017, our mission has always been to deliver genuine, high-quality Japanese language education so students and professionals can build real careers and academic futures in Japan. From your very first Hiragana worksheet to your final Certificate of Eligibility, our experienced instructors and dedicated team stay with you every step of the way.
