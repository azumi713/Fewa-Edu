About Fewa Kokusai Educational Consultancy

Fewa Kokusai Educational Consultancy Pvt. Ltd. is a premier Japanese language institute headquartered in Pokhara Metropolitan City-4, Kaski, with a representative office in Nishi Ward, Fukuoka, Japan. Registered and approved by the Government of Nepal, we were established in 2017 to provide genuine Japanese language education and consultancy services.

Our experienced instructors follow the same curriculum used in Japanese language schools, preparing students for real-life situations in Japan as well as for JLPT and NAT-TEST exams. We maintain direct partnerships with more than 50 universities, vocational schools, and language academies across major Japanese cities.
