Service Matrix

What We Do, End to End

Five foundational services carry every student from first inquiry to arrival in Japan.

Japanese Language Courses

Structured instruction from absolute beginner (N5) through advanced (N1), following the same curriculum used in Japanese language schools and preparing students for the JLPT.

Educational Counseling & Visa Support

One-on-one academic counseling paired with complete visa application support, from paperwork to interview preparation.

University & Vocational School Placement

Direct placement guidance across our network of 50+ partner universities, vocational schools, and language academies in Japan.

Documentation & Paperwork Guidance

Meticulous handling of every form and procedure, ensuring accuracy and timely submission to minimize delays.

Pre-Departure Orientation

Housing arrangements and cultural preparation so students arrive in Japan ready, confident, and settled.
